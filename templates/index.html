Interest Rate Savings

How lower 10-year Treasury rates reduce annual interest payments compared to a benchmark.

The U.S. faces a significant challenge: refinancing $9.2 trillion in government debt during 2025 alone. This massive 'wall of maturities' underscores the critical importance of borrowing costs.

As highlighted by the United States Secretary of the Treasury, Scott Bessent, even small changes matter – each basis point (0.01%) reduction in the 10-year Treasury yield can translate to roughly $1 billion in annual interest savings. This tool tracks the current rate against a recent high, illustrating the potential impact of these fluctuations.
